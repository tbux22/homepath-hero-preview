These are state agencies and nonprofit organizations. They run their own programs. HomePath does not.

Phone numbers and hours can change. Check each source before you rely on it.

    Department of Banking foreclosure hotline

    Guidance and referrals about foreclosure. Weekdays, 8 to 5.

    1-877-472-8313

    Statewide Legal Services of Connecticut

    Legal advice and referrals. There are income rules.

    1-800-453-3320

    Connecticut Judicial Branch

    Court forms and the Foreclosure Mediation Program.
